Project Overview
UX Research
STEP - 01
Target Audience
The platform targets Taiwanese high school students—as well as their teachers and parents—offering an intuitive tool to manage learning portfolios and college-prep workflows.
STEP - 02
Competitor Analysis
Existing Taiwanese admissions services focus on document storage or information lookup but lack integrated calendar planning and community-sharing features.
STEP - 03

Design Guideline

Grid

Responsive 12-column grid: desktop uses 8-col main + 4-col sidebar; tablet/mobile collapse to single-column stack. Navigation via hamburger menu or bottom bar, maintaining alignment and whitespace consistency.

Typography & Color

Typeface: Noto Sans CJK, Arial. Headings: weight 600–700; body text: 400. Line-height and letter-spacing are fine-tuned for readability. Primary color is purple with blue accents; three shades of gray serve as background and secondary text to balance brand tone and clarity.

Component

Card-based layout with 8px corner radius and 16px spacing. Buttons include solid and outlined styles with hover/disabled states. Forms and tables use 16px padding with fine borders. Icons follow a 20×20px linear style using theme colors and grayscale for interactive states.

User Interface Design

Learning Portfolio Management

Awards & Honors Archive

In the “Awards & Honors” section, students list competitions, certificates, and both in-school and external awards, attaching proof documents or images. The system auto-categorizes entries by year and type, generating an intuitive timeline and showcase page. This enables one-click export for applications and invites mentor reviews and comments, ensuring every achievement is thoroughly documented and celebrated.

Comprehensive Portfolio Integration

The “Portfolio” module supports diverse input formats—text notes, image portfolios, video links, and external file imports. Students organize entries by course, project, or self-directed learning theme. Built-in templates and layout tools help create polished, presentation-style documents quickly. Automatic cloud backups ensure the full learning journey is accessible anytime, anywhere.

User Interface Design

Campus Activity Management

Track Calendar Activities

The smart calendar auto-imports key school events and exam schedules and syncs with Google Calendar or iCal. Students can track activities directly on the calendar, set custom reminders, tags, and recurring events, and view tasks in calendar or to-do list formats—ensuring no important date is missed and time management stays effortless and organized.

Event Lookup & Management

In the “Event Hub,” students explore club registrations, on- and off-campus competitions, and seminar listings. One-click registration adds events to personal calendars. Post-registration, students access event details, mentor contacts, and location maps, then share reflections afterward, creating review threads for peer interaction and knowledge exchange.

User Interface Design

Community & College-Prep Sharing

Admissions Sharing & Community Interaction

The “Admissions Forum” hosts peer and alumni experience sharing. Students ask questions, comment, and like posts, while the system recommends trending discussions by interest and major. Professional counselors and teachers publish official announcements and resources, creating a multi-tiered interaction ecosystem where knowledge and experiences continuously circulate and grow.

School Lookup & Comparison

The “School Search” module lets students filter universities and programs by major, location, or ranking. They compare admission scores, employment rates, and student reviews. Custom wish lists and ranking tools generate tailored application recommendations, empowering students to make the best college-choice decisions.
