Danshan EScholarOnline Learning App

An innovative online English learning platform that empowers students by connecting them with top U.S. students. Through real-time interaction, video-based resources, and guided learning, it enhances English proficiency while broadening global perspectives. Experienced tutors provide full-session guidance to cultivate proactive learning and all-around English skills.

Project Overview
UX Research
STEP - 01
Target Audience
Danshan EScholar targets teens (12–20) seeking to improve spoken English, build cultural awareness, and learn independently. It combines interactive classes and quality tutors to offer a flexible, engaging experience.
STEP - 02
Competitor Analysis
Unlike platforms like VIPABC Kids or Duolingo that focus on foreign teachers or gamification, Danshan sets itself apart with elite mentors and curated video content.
STEP - 03
Design Goals
We aim to design a warm, intuitive English learning platform that’s easy for students, tutors, and parents to use. User-centered layouts and interactions help boost engagement and motivation.

Design Guideline

Typography & Color

Typography:
Roboto sans-serif; Headings 600–700 weight; body text 400; adjusted line-height and letter-spacing for better readability.
Color Palette:
Dominant yellow, accented with blue, supported by three levels of grayscale for backgrounds and secondary text — balancing brand tone and clarity.

Component

Card-based layout with 8px corner radius and 16px spacing. Buttons include solid and outlined styles with hover/disabled states. Forms and tables use 16px padding with fine borders. Icons follow a 20×20px linear style using theme colors and grayscale for interactive states.

User Interface Design

Course Management

For Students

A full set of features includes class booking, online learning, and progress evaluations — allowing learners to plan their schedule flexibly. Built-in learning history tracking and reminders support self-reflection and strategy optimization.

For Tutors (Mentors)

Mentors can prepare lessons, manage materials, and build their own teaching profile pages showcasing their expertise, teaching philosophy, and student reviews. This also helps tutors build credibility and visibility on the platform.

User Interface Design

Member Center

A centralized portal integrating user profiles, learning history, tutor interactions, notifications, and feedback. Feedback and support channels ensure transparency and efficient communication, allowing both sides to focus on effective teaching and learning.Students can:


  • ✓ Edit profiles
  • ✓ Track class progress and scores
  • ✓ Receive real-time updates
  • ✓ Follow favorite tutors
  • ✓ Manage orders and payments
User Interface Design

Try Before You Subscribe

Offers free online trial classes and teaching evaluations, allowing students to explore the course style before enrolling and receive personalized feedback and performance tracking reports after each session — making learning more focused and goal-oriented.

Free online trial lesson

New users can explore course content, teaching style, and interactivity before committing. This boosts initial trust and engagement, while serving as a showcase for tutors' personal brands.

Performance Evaluation

A built-in feedback and evaluation system provides personalized post-class reports, helping students identify strengths and areas for improvement. Tutors can tailor future lessons accordingly for better outcomes and precise learning paths.
